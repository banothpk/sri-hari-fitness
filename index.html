THE FOUNDER

MEET THE FOUNDER

FOUNDER

Praveen

Praveen founded Sri Hari Fitness with one goal: to bring a world-class training experience to Sangareddy through premium, carefully selected fitness equipment.

From the beginning, the focus was never on filling the gym with random machines. Every piece of equipment was chosen based on biomechanics, comfort, stability, range of motion, and real training effectiveness.

At Sri Hari Fitness, quality is not an afterthought—it is the foundation of everything we do.
